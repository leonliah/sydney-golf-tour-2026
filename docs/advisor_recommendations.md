## Part 3: Practical Guide for Visiting Korean Golfers

### 3.1 Cultural & Etiquette Differences: Korea vs. Australia

| Aspect | Korean Golf Culture (한국 골프 문화) | Australian Golf Realities (호주 골프 문화) | Operational Action for 4-Ball Team |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Caddies & Pacing** | Mandatory caddie manages clubs, yardages, putts, and cart driving. | **Self-guided & Unassisted.** | Use GPS watch / rangefinders; practice "Ready Golf" to maintain a **sub-4-hour round**. |
| **Carts & Fairways** | Carts strictly on paved cart paths. | **90-Degree Rule / Fairway Access** permitted on most courses (weather permitting). | Drive directly to the ball across fairways (except greens & teebox collars); saves leg fatigue. |
| **Pace of Play** | Caddie regulates pace. 4h 30m – 5h standard. | Strict club marshals enforce **3h 45m – 4h 00m**. | Continuous putting, play provisional balls for bush lies, pick up when out of strokes. |
| **Half-Way House** | 20–30 min relaxed break at the turn. | **Quick 5-minute Turn.** Grab a snack/drink from Pro Shop / Cart Bar. | No long mid-round dining break. Keep momentum going into Hole 10. |
| **Tipping** | Fixed mandatory caddie fee. | **No Tipping Expected.** | Australian green fees and cart hires are fully inclusive. |
| **Dress Code** | Flashy designer golf wear common. | Traditional golf attire strictly enforced (collared shirts, tailored shorts with sports socks, soft spikes). | Metal spikes banned. No denim, cargo shorts, or collarless t-shirts. |

### 3.2 Physical, Heat & Logistics Defense Checklist
1. **Extreme Summer UV (Index 11–13+):** Stock **SPF 50+ sunscreen**, UV hats, and ice towels; reapply at Hole 10.
2. **Hydration & Electrolytes:** Supply 2.0L–2.5L of water/electrolytes per golfer per round.
3. **NSW GC Cart Verification (`NSW-53ZR560E`):** Booked as a motorized cart round; verify receipt cart terms prior to arrival.
4. **Vehicle Requirement:** Strictly **8-seat `2-3-3` bench** (Kia Carnival / Hyundai Staria) with 3rd row folded flat for 5 pax + 4 golf bags + 4 large suitcases.
